# Beta Testing — Sprint 1

Recruit **5–10 testers** across a spread of experience: 2–3 beginners, 2–3 active
intraday/swing traders, 1–2 investors. Ideally people who'd plausibly pay ₹1,200/mo.

**Do not ask "do you like it?"** — it produces polite, useless answers. Ask what they
did, what confused them, and whether they'd pay. Behaviour and money, not opinions.

## Before they start (setup)
- [ ] Give them a real account (or the signup link) — watch the signup flow itself.
- [ ] Ask them to use it on the **device they'd actually use** (most on mobile).
- [ ] **Don't guide them.** Watch (or ask them to screen-record) the first 3 minutes silently.
- [ ] Give one open task: *"It's the morning before market open — figure out what to watch today."*
- [ ] Confirm analytics is capturing their session (Clarity recording).

## The 7 core questions (ask verbatim, after they've used it)
1. **What confused you?** (anything you had to stop and figure out)
2. **What did you expect but couldn't find?**
3. **Which page did you use most?**
4. **Which page did you never open?** (and did you know it existed?)
5. **Did the Command Center help you decide what to do?** How, or why not?
6. **Would you pay ₹1,200/month today? Why or why not?** (push for the real reason)
7. **What nearly made you leave?** (the moment of most friction or doubt)

## Probes (only if they're vague)
- On the Command Center: *"In the first 15 seconds, did you know what deserved your attention?"*
- On the NBA "Start here" card: *"Was that actually the thing you'd do next?"*
- On the watchlist: *"Was there a reason to come back to it tomorrow?"*
- On price: *"What would need to be true for ₹1,200 to feel obviously worth it?"*

## Per-tester capture template
Copy this block per tester into the observation log or a sheet.

```

Q2 Expected but missing: ____

Q5 Command Center helped? ____
Q6 Pay ₹1200? YES / NO — real reason: ____
Q7 Nearly left when: ____

```

## What we're really testing
- **Activation:** does a first-timer reach "aha" (know what to watch) fast?
- **The home:** is the Command Center the right front door, or do they gravitate elsewhere?
- **Willingness to pay:** the only opinion that counts — and *the reason* behind it.
- **Leak points:** the exact moment friction almost lost them.
